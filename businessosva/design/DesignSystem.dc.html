Business OS for Van Amstel Business Tech · Design system v1

Foundations & components

A restrained, neutral-first system for a dense business tool. Colour is reserved for meaning — status, priority, money and the one primary action on a screen.

Neutral first

Warm greys carry 95% of the UI.

Colour means something

Never decorative. Always a state.

Dense, not cramped

13px base, 4px grid, generous rows.

Everything links

Every entity name is a way in.
